# Assess requirement evidence

Read [requirements and assessment](../define-requirements/references/requirements.context.md) and, for
commissioned deliveries, [specifications](../define-requirements/references/specifications.context.md).

Identify the exact obligations and revisions being assessed, their acceptance
criteria, applicable verification plan and available observations. Check scope,
conditions, versions and limitations before using evidence. A planned or
implemented check is not an observed result; a coverage link is not a pass.

Compare observations with the required demonstrations. Keep failed, blocked,
skipped and unrun checks distinct. Report measured values separately from targets
and binding thresholds. Human observations may provide valid evidence; automation
alone does not establish sufficiency. Do not infer whole-requirement satisfaction
from one passing check when other required demonstrations are missing.

Assess combined outcomes at the commissioning boundary. Child results and
conformity claims support this assessment but do not replace it. Distinguish the
assessment you can support from an acceptance decision reserved to an owner.

Retain historical evidence against its original revision. For changed obligations,
assess whether prior evidence remains applicable and identify new observations
needed; never silently relabel historical results. Record findings and unresolved
gaps in the project's evidence records, separately from definitions and plans.

This skill does not prescribe a universal result schema, automatic acceptance
rule or evidence dashboard. Use the project's agreed procedures and preserve
unknowns rather than inventing acceptance semantics.
